## 2. Training Instructions (English)
**Introduction:**
"Welcome to the Underground Gas Leak Response Module. A toxic and explosive gas leak (Carbon Monoxide and Methane) has been detected in this sector. You must follow standard emergency procedures to safely evacuate to the Refuge Chamber."

**Step Instructions:**
1. **Detection:** "Check your portable gas monitor immediately upon observing the visual haze."
2. **Hazard ID:** "Identify the primary hazard type."
3. **PPE Selection:** "Select and equip the appropriate breathing apparatus from the PPE rack."
4. **Alarm Activation:** "Pull the emergency alarm lever to warn your colleagues."
5. **Airflow Procedure:** "Check the windsock. You must travel UPWIND, against the flow of the gas."
6. **Evacuation:** "Proceed directly to the marked Safe Zone waypoint."
7. **Completion:** "Enter the Refuge Chamber and secure the door."

## 3. Critical Safety Knowledge
- **Why SCBA?** Dust masks filter particulates but offer zero protection against toxic gases like CO or oxygen displacement. A Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus (SCBA) or Filter Self-Rescuer (FSR) is mandatory.
- **Why Upwind?** Traveling downwind forces you to walk within the gas plume, prolonging exposure and increasing asphyxiation risk.
- **Why No Open Flames?** Methane ($CH_4$) is highly explosive at 5-15% concentration in the air. Non-intrinsically safe electronics or open flames will cause an immediate explosion.
